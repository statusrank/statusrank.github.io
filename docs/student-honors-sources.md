# Student honors

Verified on 2026-10-22. Display only honors earned during graduate study.
The bilingual roster is maintained in `_data/students.yml` and rendered by
`_includes/current-students.html`. New honors can be added without duplicating
the English and Chinese student markup. The first three honors are visible;
all full award names, including additional records, are available through an
accessible native disclosure on every card with honors. Current-student
cards use the same portrait-and-content arrangement as former-student cards,
in two desktop columns and one when content space is tighter. A portrait sits
on the left with names, role, and awards grouped on the right, following the
user's preferred former-student reference. Column count follows the available content width rather than only the
viewport, so the profile sidebar cannot squeeze three cards into a narrow area.
Compact award labels wrap as needed; their full names and years remain in
the tooltip and accessible label. Per the user's position request, publication
counts sit at the top right beside the portrait/name header, one compact badge
per venue. Honor badges stay in the right-hand content area below the header
and wrap naturally. Both types use the same height, type scale, capsule shape,
and the established restrained blue-lilac palette. Publication badges use a
soft violet fill and stronger type; honor badges retain the quieter blue-gray
treatment. No venue-by-venue rainbow palette is used.

Three independent visual-design, frontend, and typography reviews on 2026-10-22
informed the initial compact design. The user's later former-student reference
supersedes the small-tile layout; its horizontal arrangement and capsule badges
are the design baseline for future additions. Retained refinements: a readable shared
badge scale, stable English-name line breaks, restrained initial avatars,
dark-theme link/focus contrast, and keyboard/touch access to full honor names.
Current and former students share badge height, font size, and corner radius.
Final local verification passed the Jekyll build and bilingual roster checks:
12 current students, 5 portraits, 6 graduate honors, and 3 former students.
Browser checks at seven widths from 320 to 1920 pixels found no horizontal
overflow; all current publication/honor badges measured the same height.
English mobile keyboard expansion and the Chinese light/dark views were checked.
